# French press extraction notes

The French press uses immersion brewing with a metal mesh filter. Coffee
grounds steep in hot water for 4 minutes before plunging.

## Grind size

Coarse grind is essential. Fine grounds slip through the mesh and produce
sediment in the cup. Aim for a particle size similar to coarse sea salt.

## Water temperature

Brew at 95 degrees Celsius for full extraction. Lower temperatures
(85-90 C) produce lighter, more tea-like cups. Boiling water (100 C)
overextracts and pulls bitter compounds.

## Ratio

Standard ratio is 1:15 coffee to water by weight. For a single cup using
20 grams of coffee, that's 300 grams of water. Stronger cups push to 1:12;
lighter cups go 1:17.

## Plunge timing

Plunge slowly over 15 seconds. A fast plunge agitates the slurry and over-
extracts. Pour immediately after plunging to avoid continued extraction.
